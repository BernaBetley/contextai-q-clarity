# llm-facts.txt - Atomic facts about ContextAIQ

## Organization
- ContextAIQ is the practice brand.
- Prova is the product line.
- Resultado Fidalgo is the operating entity.
- ContextAIQ sells to Portuguese SMEs.
- Website is https://www.contextaiq.com
- Email is hello@contextaiq.com

## Commercial model
- Fees are hybrid: setup plus success on measured units.
- Diagnóstico is paid. There is no free strategy workshop.
- Success fees apply to incremental units versus a written baseline.
- Pre-existing volume is not billed as success.

## Products
- Prova Diagnóstico maps one process and proposes a unit of result.
- Prova Conversão is an agent on a commercial process. Setup plus euro per incremental conversion.
- Prova Backoffice is an agent on an admin process. Setup plus euro per completed unit.
- Prova Suporte is an agent on support. Setup plus euro per resolved ticket.
- Prova Visibilidade is a nested €500 AI visibility audit (20 queries, 4 LLMs, 3 competitors, 5-7 business days).

## Time-to-production
- Typical path from licensed ChatGPT to one production agent is 30-90 days on a single process.
