## What ContextAI Q Measures
- AI Share-of-Voice: how often a brand appears in AI-generated responses.
- Citation Rate: how frequently LLMs attribute information to a brand's sources.
- Accuracy Score: correctness of information presented about a brand.
- Hallucination Flags: false or fabricated information about a brand.
- Competitive Displacement: queries where competitors appear but the brand doesn't.

## Methodology
- ContextAI Q uses a structured, repeatable methodology.
- The methodology includes query selection, multi-LLM testing, visibility scoring, accuracy assessment, competitive benchmarking, root cause analysis, and recommendation development.
- Visibility scoring uses a 0-100 scale.
- Visibility scoring comprises: appearance (0-40), prominence (0-30), accuracy (0-30).
